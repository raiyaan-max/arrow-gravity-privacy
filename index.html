Arrow Gravity — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Arrow Gravity is an offline puzzle game developed and published by XinLabs. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping the app simple and privacy-friendly.

Information We Collect

Arrow Gravity does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Arrow Gravity stores limited game-progress information locally on your device, such as completed levels and unlocked progress.

This information is used only to provide the game's progress and functionality. It is not transmitted to XinLabs or any third party.

Internet and Network Access

Arrow Gravity is designed to operate offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

The app does not request internet/network access.

Advertising and Analytics

Arrow Gravity does not currently contain advertising, third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or behavioral tracking.

Third-Party Services

Arrow Gravity does not currently integrate third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Children's Privacy

Arrow Gravity does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Arrow Gravity's privacy practices, please contact XinLabs through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
